## 3. KEY EMPIRICAL FINDINGS

1. The $2^\Delta$ Mathematical Rule Confirmed:
   - Increasing bcrypt cost factor from 10 to 12 represents a 2-round increase ($2^{12} / 2^{10} = 4$).
   - Measured Hashcat GPU speed dropped from 1,393 H/s to 387 H/s ($1393 / 387 = 3.60\times$ reduction, closely matching theoretical 4×).
2. Defense Asymmetry (MD5 vs. bcrypt):
   - Switching from unsalted MD5 to bcrypt rounds=12 reduces GPU cracking throughput by a massive factor of ~178,000× (68.85 Million H/s down to 387 H/s).
   - A dictionary search that takes a fraction of a second against MD5 is pushed to half a day on an RTX 3060 Ti and over a month on a CPU.
